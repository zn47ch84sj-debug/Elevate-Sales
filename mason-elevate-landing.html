Do I need sales experience?

No. We train you on everything: the pitch, handling objections and closing. What we look for is drive, coachability and a strong work ethic.

Is this a summer job or year-round?

Both options exist. Pest control teams run a seasonal summer push, while fiber internet teams sell year-round. We’ll help you figure out which fits your schedule.

What about housing and travel?

Many teams sell in markets away from home. Housing and travel details vary by team and market — we’ll walk you through the specifics during your call before you commit to anything.

How does pay work?

Pay is commission-based, so your income is tied to your results. Full pay structure, hours and day-to-day life are covered on the intro call.
